Aves-Next

A modern home for your media.

Aves-Next is a community-maintained continuation of Aves: an Android visual media gallery and metadata explorer built with Flutter, with deep media, metadata, location, map, and video capabilities.

The app

Built around exploring a real media library.

Aves-Next keeps the core strengths of Aves while continuing under a separate project identity and Android distribution path.

Browse collections

Explore albums, tags, places, countries, maps, search, and filtering across large photo and video libraries.

Inspect metadata

Work with the information embedded in image and video files and surface detailed media metadata.

Understand location

Use geographic views and mapping features to connect media with places, paths, and coordinates.

Capabilities

A broad gallery and media toolset.

These are project capabilities inherited from the Aves codebase and retained in Aves-Next's application.

Media galleryFast browsing of photo and video collections.
Collection navigationAlbums, tags, places, countries, maps, search, and filters.
Advanced media formatsSupport for many image/video formats including panoramic and 360° media.
Media managementFile operations, collection selection, bin/recycle workflows, and related actions.
Viewer toolsPlayback controls, metadata overlays, slideshow behavior, and viewer settings.
Android integrationShortcuts, global search, media viewer/picker, screen saver handling, and Android TV support.
Under the hood

Flutter on Android, split into focused modules.

The repository uses local packages to separate concerns such as maps, video, UI, services, utilities, reporting, and model code.

Android distribution

Two supported release variants.

The current supported build path is centered on Libre and Izzy variants. Firebase and Google Play Services are not part of the supported distribution path.
